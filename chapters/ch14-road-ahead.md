# Chapter 14: The Road Ahead

## Trends to watch
- **Longer autonomous runs**: agents working for hours on larger tasks, making checkpoints and logs more important.
- **Agents testing on real devices and emulators**, driving UIs the way users do.
- **Spec-driven development** as the default, with specs versioned like code.
- **On-device AI**: models running on the phone itself, changing privacy and offline design.
- **Agent-native apps**: apps that expose actions to assistants and agents, not only to touch interfaces.
- **Standards for connecting agents to tools and data**, which reduce lock-in.

## What stays the same
Users want apps that work, respect privacy, and feel good. Accountability stays human. Clear thinking about requirements remains the scarce skill.

## A caution about this chapter
Tools and capabilities change fast. Treat specific products in this book as examples; revisit them before publishing.

## Doubt Box
- **Will ADLC be outdated in two years?** Specific tools will change; the principles (clarify, small verifiable units, independent verification, human gates) are likely to last.
- **What should I learn now?** Spec writing, code review, testing strategy, and measurement.
